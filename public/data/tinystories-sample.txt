Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to explore the forest near her home.
One sunny day, Lily found a tiny rabbit sitting under a mushroom. "Hello," said Lily softly. The rabbit twitched its nose and hopped closer.
Together they walked to a sparkling stream. Lily shared her apple, and the rabbit drank cool water. They became best friends.
When the sun began to set, Lily waved goodbye. The rabbit promised to wait for her tomorrow. And they lived happily with many more adventures.
